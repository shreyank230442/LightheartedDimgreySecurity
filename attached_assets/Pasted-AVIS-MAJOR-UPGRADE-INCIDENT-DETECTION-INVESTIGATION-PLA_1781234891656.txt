AVIS MAJOR UPGRADE - INCIDENT DETECTION & INVESTIGATION PLATFORM

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT redesign the UI.

Do NOT remove existing functionality.

Do NOT break currently working features.

The current system behaves like an object detection demo.

The goal is to transform AVIS into an AI-powered incident detection and investigation platform capable of analyzing CCTV footage and identifying real-world incidents such as fights, road accidents, fires, explosions, and violent events.

====================================================================
PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
=================

The system should support the following workflow:

Upload CCTV Video
↓
Analyze Entire Video
↓
Detect Incidents
↓
Generate Incident Timeline
↓
Jump To Incident Timestamp
↓
Inspect Evidence Frame
↓
Apply Image Processing Pipeline
↓
Review Incident Details
↓
Generate Investigation Report

The focus must be INCIDENTS rather than objects.

====================================================================
TARGET INCIDENT TYPES
=====================

The platform should prioritize detection of:

1. Road Accidents

Vehicle collisions

Vehicle overturning

Pedestrian impact

Vehicle entering wrong lane

Sudden crash events

2. Fights / Violence

Physical altercations

Aggressive movements

Multiple people fighting

Crowd violence

3. Fire Incidents

Visible flames

Fire outbreaks

Smoke detection

4. Explosions

Explosion flash

Rapid fireball expansion

Sudden large-area brightness changes

Explosion aftermath

5. Suspicious Activities

Running away after incident

Erratic movement

Panic movement

Unusual activity patterns

6. Emergency Events

Crowd panic

Mass movement

People collapsing

Dangerous situations

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
====================

1. Preserve existing functionality.

2. Maintain FastAPI architecture.

3. Use OpenCV for processing.

4. Prefer YOLOv8n or YOLOv8 ONNX Runtime.

5. Eliminate random frame selection.

6. Use analyst-selected frames for investigation.

7. Prioritize incidents rather than simple object detection.

8. The system should resemble an AI-powered incident investigation platform rather than a surveillance analytics dashboard.

Before implementation, inspect current architecture and determine the safest migration path while preserving all working features.
